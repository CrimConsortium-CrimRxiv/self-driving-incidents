What coverage reports

A 2015 Tesla Model S struck a parked Snohomish County sheriff's patrol vehicle in Arlington, Washington, while a deputy was attending an earlier collision with his emergency lights on. Washington State Patrol said the driver told troopers the car was in Autopilot mode and that he assumed it would slow and move over. No one was injured; the sheriff's department reported significant damage to the patrol car.
