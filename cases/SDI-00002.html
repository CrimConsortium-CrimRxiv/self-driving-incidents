What coverage reports

A Tesla Model 3 left the road in Katy, Texas, on a Friday night and struck a house, killing a 76-year-old woman. The driver told Harris County sheriff's deputies the car was on Autopilot. A Tesla vice president wrote that the driver had overridden the system by pressing the accelerator fully, and the NTSB's preliminary findings, as reported a month later, also said the driver overrode the driver-assistance system. NHTSA opened a special investigation, and the sheriff's office said it would present its findings to the district attorney.
