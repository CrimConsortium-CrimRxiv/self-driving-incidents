What coverage reports

In 2023 a Tesla Model Y struck and killed a 71-year-old woman who had got out of her car on an Arizona highway and was waving traffic around an earlier crash caused by sun glare. Coverage ties the crash to Tesla's Full Self-Driving (Supervised) system. Her daughter's lawsuit against Tesla was settled on undisclosed terms in June 2026. The crash is one of four that prompted an NHTSA investigation of the system in low visibility, which was escalated to an engineering analysis in March 2026.
