What coverage reports

On April 25, 2019, a 2019 Tesla Model S with Autopilot engaged ran a stop sign at about 62 mph at a T-intersection on Card Sound Road near Key Largo, Florida, and struck a parked SUV, which hit two people standing nearby; one was killed and one seriously injured. The driver, who had looked down to retrieve a dropped phone, was cited for careless driving and pleaded no contest, with adjudication withheld. In August 2025 a federal jury found Tesla 33% responsible; Tesla's share of the judgment is about $243 million. Tesla filed an appeal brief on November 6, 2026, arguing the distracted driver caused the crash; the appeals court has not ruled.
