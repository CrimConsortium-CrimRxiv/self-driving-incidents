What coverage reports

Reports describe a Tesla Model S with Autopilot engaged traveling through a stop sign at a Key Largo intersection and striking a parked Tahoe; the impact sent the vehicle into two people standing nearby, killing one and seriously injuring the other. The driver said he trusted Autopilot too much, while Tesla argued he ignored warnings and misused the system. A jury found Tesla partly responsible, and the company is appealing the judgment.
