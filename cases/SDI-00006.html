What coverage reports

Late on September 12, 2026, a 2024 Tesla Model Y left Serrano Parkway in El Dorado Hills, California, hit a tree and caught fire; three of the four people inside were killed and the fourth survived with major injuries. The California Highway Patrol said the car left the road for unknown reasons and the cause remains under investigation. Electrek reported in November that Tesla's own crash report to NHTSA lists the driver-assistance feature as verified engaged with a pre-crash speed of 74 mph, with the narrative redacted. The report does not show which feature was on or what caused the crash.
