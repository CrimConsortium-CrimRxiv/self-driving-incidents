Tesla on Autopilot/FSD hit an overturned truck at 71 mph, killing 3

What coverage reports

Just before 4 a.m. on May 31, 2026, a 2023 Tesla Model 3 struck an overturned box truck on southbound I-75 near Naples, Florida, and a man who had stopped to help the truck's driver. Two of the Tesla's passengers and the man who stopped were killed; the Tesla's driver and two other passengers were seriously hurt. A Florida Highway Patrol trooper said the driver would have seen only the truck's unlit underside. Electrek reported in November that Tesla's own crash report to NHTSA lists the driver-assistance feature as verified engaged with a pre-crash speed of 71 mph, with the narrative redacted.
