Tesla on autopilot crashed into 2 trucks in Somerset County while driver did her makeup

What coverage reports

At about 11:28 a.m. on November 2, 2026, a 2022 Tesla Model Y heading north on Route 206 in Montgomery Township, New Jersey, crossed the centre line and struck a tractor-trailer and then a garbage truck. Police said the car was being operated in Autopilot mode and the driver was applying makeup; one report says investigators concluded she probably grabbed the steering wheel while shifting in her seat, which stopped Autopilot. The two truck drivers had minor injuries and the Tesla driver was unhurt. Police issued the driver summonses for careless driving, reckless driving and failure to maintain lane.
