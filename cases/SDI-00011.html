Woman arrested after self-driving Tesla crashes into construction equipment while she was allegedly intoxicated in western Indiana

What coverage reports

At about 10:30 p.m. on November 19, 2026, a 2016 Tesla struck two arrow boards in a construction zone on eastbound I-70 near mile marker 13 in Vigo County, Indiana. Indiana State Police said their investigation found the car was in self-driving mode; no product was named and one outlet describes the mode as alleged. No one was hurt, though workers were nearby. The driver, 57, was arrested after a test showed a blood alcohol content of 0.14% and was charged with three offences related to intoxicated driving and a highway work zone.
