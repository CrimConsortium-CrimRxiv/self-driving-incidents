What coverage reports

Videos posted on X on November 12, 2026, appear to show a Tesla moving out of the path of a vehicle approaching from behind at high speed in Pleasant Grove, Utah, around November 4 or 5. The person who posted them, who was not in the car, said the Tesla was in self-driving mode. The report says the available information does not establish whether the system or the driver made the manoeuvre, and that police information on a serious collision there does not identify a Tesla as involved. The driver of the speeding vehicle was reported to be in extremely critical condition.
