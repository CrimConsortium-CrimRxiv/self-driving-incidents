What coverage reports

On February 18, 2023, a Tesla Model S struck a fire truck parked across Interstate 680 in Contra Costa County, California. The driver was killed and a passenger in the car was seriously injured. The family's lawsuit said Autopilot had been active before the crash and argued that the system and its marketing were deficient; Tesla contested the claims and said the driver had ignored warnings. Tesla later settled the lawsuit, and the reports say a judge had allowed the family's claims to proceed.
