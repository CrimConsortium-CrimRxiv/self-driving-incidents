What coverage reports

Electrek reports that a 2024 Tesla Model Y left Interstate 35 in Daviess County, Missouri, struck a guardrail, and its driver died. It says Tesla’s report to federal regulators recorded the driver-assist system as engaged, but the system and software details were redacted. The report says the engagement does not by itself establish that the system caused the crash.
