What coverage reports

Electrek reports that a Tesla Model X and a three-wheeled motorcycle were involved in a collision at a Moraine intersection on July 23, 2025. The report says the motorcyclist died and the other driver had non-life-threatening injuries; the cause was left under investigation. Electrek matched the incident to Tesla’s report to federal regulators, which marked the driver-assist system “Verified Engaged” based on telematics, but did not identify which system was active.
