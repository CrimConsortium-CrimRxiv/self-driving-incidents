What coverage reports

The reports say that on April 7 in Plano, a Tesla moved through a lowered railroad crossing gate as a DART train approached, breaking the gate and shattering the windshield. The driver said Full Self-Driving was engaged and described the vehicle accelerating forward; one report says he had zoned out while waiting. The driver was not injured, and Tesla took possession of the vehicle and said it was investigating what happened.
