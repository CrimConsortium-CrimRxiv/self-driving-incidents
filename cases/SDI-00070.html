What coverage reports

A Tesla Model 3 left the pavement on Upper Bear Creek Road in Colorado in May 2022, struck a tree and caught fire; the driver died and the passenger was severely injured. The lawsuit alleged Autopilot was engaged and steered the car off the road, while Tesla says vehicle data showed Autopilot was inactive. Tesla also argued that the driver's intoxication caused the loss of control; the coverage reports that Tesla filed a motion to dismiss the lawsuit.
