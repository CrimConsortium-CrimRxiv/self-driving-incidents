What coverage reports

According to a court filing, on September 13, 2025, a driver in Hangzhou activated the car's assisted-driving function and used a device to evade its monitoring. The filing says the driver moved to the passenger seat and fell asleep while the car continued driving, then the system disengaged and the vehicle stopped on a local road. The report says the courts convicted the driver of driving under the influence and tampering with the system, and imposed a fine and a prison sentence.
