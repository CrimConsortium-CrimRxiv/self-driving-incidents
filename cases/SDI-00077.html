What coverage reports

The reports describe a Tesla Model S striking a motorcyclist on State Route 522 in Snohomish County in April 2024; the motorcyclist died. The driver told a trooper he was using Autopilot and looking at his phone, and internal Tesla data reportedly showed the feature had been enabled shortly before the crash. The driver was arrested but was not charged, and the motorcyclist’s family filed a wrongful-death lawsuit alleging that Tesla’s system failed to detect the motorcycle.
