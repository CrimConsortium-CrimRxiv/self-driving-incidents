Self-driving incidents: claimed, questioned, confirmed.

An open dataset of crashes, near misses, police stops and court cases in which a vehicle's automated-driving feature is claimed, questioned or confirmed to be involved. Each incident is one case, built from published reports and coded for what happened and who says so. The dataset covers Tesla for now.

We're iteratively improving this site and dataset. Please email corrections or suggestions to consortium@crimrxiv.com.

Compiled from published news reports found through Google News and Bing News searches, refreshed daily (how cases are sampled). Coded values are drafted by a language model from those reports and are not yet reviewed by a person; each one links to the passage it rests on. A count of incidents is not a rate, and a mention of an automated-driving feature is not a finding that it was in use. Verify every case at its sources.
